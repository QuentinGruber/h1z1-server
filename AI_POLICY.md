## Disclosure

Any use of AI to contribute to h1emu must be disclosed, with context about how it was used.

## No AI-authored commits

Only humans can be commit authors or listed as Co-authored-by.

## No AI-generated communication

We want to talk to you, not a machine. Do not paste AI-generated output directly into conversations with humans, no matter where they take place.

AI may be used to assist with translation and accessibility purposes, but please include the original untranslated text as well.

## No abusive use of AI in issues / PR descriptions
  
AI may be used, but not abusively to generate walls of text nobody wants to read. Stay concise and genuinely useful.

## Ownership

As a contributor, you are responsible for everything you submit. You must personally understand every line of code or documentation that you put forward for review, and be able to articulate the design rationale and implementation tradeoffs when asked. Work within your skill level and knowledge of the project.

## Review

AI-assisted PRs will be held to the same standard of review: correctness, tests, rationale, incremental reviewable units and appropriate design.
